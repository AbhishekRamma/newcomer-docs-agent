## What a SIN is
A Social Insurance Number is a nine-digit number that you need in order to work in Canada or to get government benefits and programs.

## How long a SIN lasts
A SIN given to a Canadian citizen or a permanent resident is permanent and does not expire.
A SIN that starts with 9 is given to temporary residents and has an expiry date that matches their immigration document.

## Protecting your SIN
Only share your SIN when the law requires it, such as with your employer or the tax agency.

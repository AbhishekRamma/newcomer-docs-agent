## Time limit
You must finish the graduated licensing steps within five years.

## G1 waiting time
With a G1 you wait 12 months before the G2 road test, or eight months if you pass an approved driver education course.

## G1 conditions
Your blood alcohol level must be zero. You cannot drive between midnight and 5 a.m.
You cannot drive on 400 series highways, and a passenger with at least four years of driving experience must sit beside you.

## Waiting period
Ontario has removed the three-month wait. There is no waiting period, so eligible people are covered immediately once they qualify.

## Physical presence
To qualify you must spend at least 153 days of every 12-month period physically in Ontario.
You also need to be in Ontario for 153 of your first 183 days after you become a resident.

## If you are found ineligible
If ServiceOntario decides you are not eligible, you can ask for a review of the decision.
After that you can appeal to the Health Services Appeal and Review Board. Some cases can also go to the OHIP Eligibility Review Committee (OERC).

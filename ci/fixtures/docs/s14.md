## Your first return
As a resident of Canada you report income from outside Canada that you earned after you arrived.
Newcomers who arrived in 2025 file their 2025 return by April 30, 2026.

## Without a SIN
Contact the CRA for a temporary tax number (TTN) if you are not able to get a SIN.

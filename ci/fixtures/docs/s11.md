## Income to report
For the part of the year after you became a resident, you report your world income, including income from outside Canada.

## Moving expenses
Generally, you cannot deduct the costs of moving to Canada.
An exception applies to full-time students who moved to study and who received a scholarship, bursary, fellowship or research grant.

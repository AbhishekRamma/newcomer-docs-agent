## Benefits before your first return
Newcomers can apply for benefit payments before they file their first tax return, using form RC151 or form RC66.

## Canada Groceries and Essentials Benefit
A single person can receive up to $679 a year from the Canada Groceries and Essentials Benefit.
A new resident without children applies with form RC151, or online through the CRA web form.

## No SIN
If Service Canada cannot give you a SIN, you can ask the Canada Revenue Agency (CRA) for a temporary tax number (TTN) to file your return and apply for benefits.

## First return deadline
If you became a resident in 2025, your first return for 2025 is due on April 30, 2026.

## Primary documents
Bring an original primary document that proves your identity and status, for example a work permit, a study permit or a permanent resident card.

## Documents in another language
If a document is not in English or French, you need a translation by a certified translator together with an affidavit from the person who did the translation.
Family members cannot do the translation, even if they are certified.

## Identification
Show one original piece of ID that has your name and address, or that has your name and date of birth, from the list of accepted documents.
Another option is two pieces of ID from different sources.

## If the bank says no
A bank may refuse to open an account in some cases. If it refuses, it must give you written notice of the refusal.
The bank must keep records related to the refusal for 7 years.

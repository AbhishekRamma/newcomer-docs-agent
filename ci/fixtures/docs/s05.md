## Dental care
OHIP does not pay for routine dental care that you get in a dentist's office.
Some dental surgery is covered only when it has to be done in a hospital.

## Prescription drugs
OHIP does not cover prescription drugs that you take outside of hospital.
Some people can get help through the Ontario Drug Benefit program instead.

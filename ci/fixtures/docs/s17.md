## Where to go
Visit a DriveTest centre to exchange your licence. Some licences can only be exchanged at the DriveTest centre at Bay and College in Toronto.
You will take a vision test at the centre.

## Countries with an exchange agreement
Ontario has agreements with places such as Japan, Australia, France, Germany, Great Britain and Switzerland, and with all U.S. states.

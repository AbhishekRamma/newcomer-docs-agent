## Cost
Applying for a SIN is free. Service Canada does not charge a fee.

## Ways to apply
You can apply online, in person at a Service Canada office, or by mail.

## Temporary residents
A temporary resident applies with a primary document such as a work permit or a study permit that says the holder may work in Canada.
A visitor record can also be used when it states that the person may accept employment.

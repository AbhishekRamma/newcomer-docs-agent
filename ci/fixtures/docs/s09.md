## Low-cost accounts
Several banks have agreed to offer a low-cost account that charges no more than $4 a month.
There is no minimum balance requirement for this kind of account.

## No-cost accounts
Some people, such as youth and seniors who get the Guaranteed Income Supplement, can get an account with no monthly fee.

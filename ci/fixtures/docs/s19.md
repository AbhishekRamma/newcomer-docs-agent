## Failing a road test
If you fail a road test you must wait at least 10 days before you try again.

## Exchange agreements
Licences from Japan, Australia and other partner places, as well as U.S. licences, can be exchanged.

## G1 waiting time
The wait is 12 months, or 8 months after an approved driver education course.
